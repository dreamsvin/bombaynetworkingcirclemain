More Than Networking.

Most networking events end with exchanged contacts.

BNC exists to create meaningful conversations that lead to collaborations, businesses, opportunities, friendships, and long-term relationships.

Why BNC Exists

Isha Ostwal

Founder, Bombay Networking Circle

"I attended dozens of startup meetups and networking dinners, only to walk away with a pocket full of business cards and no real connection. I realized the system was broken."

"We didn't need another directory or generic social network. We needed curated rooms where trust is established instantly, leading to co-founders meeting, startups getting built, and genuine friendships forming."

Our Mission

To connect 100,000 ambitious builders across India and catalyze 10,000 new business collaborations.

The Problem

Most networking events end with exchanged contacts, transaction-first pitches, and zero meaningful outcomes. It wastes time and creates noise.

Why We Started

BNC was created to build high-trust rooms. We design environments that encourage authentic conversations first, helping builders organically form co-founding teams and secure opportunities.

The Vision

Build India's most active and valuable founder-builder ecosystem, turning casual meetings into collaborative empires.
